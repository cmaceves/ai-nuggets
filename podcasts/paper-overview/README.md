# SARS-CoV-2: A History Run-Through

A chronological walk through the SARS-CoV-2 literature, 2020 to the present —
one landmark paper per episode, in the order it was published, with a focus on
genomics. Every episode covers a paper from *Cell*, *Science*, or a Nature-family
journal.

New episodes Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

**Subscribe:**
```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cmaceves/ai-nuggets/main/podcasts/paper-overview/feed.xml
```

The episode queue lives in `papers.md`, in publication order. See `PROMPT.md`
for the editorial brief and `show.toml` for technical configuration.
